
Assignment Name: Detection Brainstorm
Aim:
List 5 uses of face/object detection and design one solution.

5 Uses of Face/Object Detection:

1.Security & Surveillance:-
Used in CCTV cameras to detect suspicious people or activities.
2.Face Unlock in Smartphones:-
Helps unlock phones by recognizing the user’s face.
3.Attendance Systems:-
Automatically marks attendance in schools or offices using face detection.
4.Self-Driving Cars:-
Detects pedestrians, vehicles, traffic signs, and obstacles.
5.Medical Imaging:-
Detects diseases like tumors in X-rays or scans.

Designed Solution:

Title: Smart Classroom Attendance System

Problem:
Manual attendance takes time and can be inaccurate.

Solution:
A face detection system installed in the classroom camera automatically identifies students and marks their attendance.

How it Works:
Camera captures student faces
System compares faces with stored database
Marks attendance automatically
Generates daily attendance report

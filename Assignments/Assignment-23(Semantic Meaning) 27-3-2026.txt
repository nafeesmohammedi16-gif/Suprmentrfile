
Assignment Name: Semantic Meaning
Aim:
Find 5 word pairs and explain semantic similarity.
Objective:
To understand semantic similarity by identifying and analyzing word pairs with similar meanings.

1. Car – Automobile:
These two words have almost identical meanings. Both refer to a vehicle used for transportation. This is an example of synonyms (high semantic similarity).

2. Teacher – Instructor:
Both words describe a person who teaches others. While “teacher” is more common in schools and “instructor” is used in formal or specific training, their meanings are very similar.

3. Happy – Joyful:
These words express positive emotions. “Joyful” is a stronger form of “happy,” but both represent a similar feeling of happiness.

4. Big – Large:
Both words describe size. They are used to indicate something of great size, making them semantically similar.

5. Fast – Quick:
These words describe speed. Both indicate something happening in a short amount of time, so they are closely related in meaning.

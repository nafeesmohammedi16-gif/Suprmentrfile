
Assignment Name: Prompt Engineer
Aim:
Write prompts for resume, business idea, study plan and compare weak vs strong prompts.
Objective:
To understand how to design effective prompts by comparing weak and strong prompts for generating better and more accurate AI outputs.

1. Resume Prompt:
 Weak Prompt:
“Make a resume for me.”

Problem: Too vague, no details → poor output

Strong Prompt:
“Create a professional resume for a B.Tech student specializing in Computer Science with skills in Python, Machine Learning, and Web Development. Include sections: summary, education, skills, projects, and internships. Keep it concise and ATS-friendly.”

2. Business Idea Prompt:
 Weak Prompt:
“Give me a business idea.”

Problem: No target, no context

Strong Prompt:
“Suggest 3 low-investment business ideas suitable for a college student in India, focusing on online or tech-based solutions. Include startup cost, target audience, and expected profit potential.”

3. Study Plan Prompt:
Weak Prompt:
“Make a study plan.”

Problem: No subject, no timeline

Strong Prompt:
“Create a 30-day study plan to learn Python from beginner to intermediate level. Include daily topics, practice exercises, and mini-projects. Assume 2 hours of study per day.”

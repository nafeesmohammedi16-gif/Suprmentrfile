Mini Project Proposal:
Title: Customer Segmentation using K-Means Clustering

1.Problem Statement:
In shopping malls or retail businesses, customers have different spending behaviors. It is difficult for businesses to target all customers effectively.
The problem is to group customers into different segments based on their characteristics so that businesses can understand and target them better.

2.Dataset:
Dataset Name: Mall Customer Dataset
Attributes:
 Customer ID
 Gender
 Age
 Annual Income (k$)
 Spending Score (1–100)
Source: Public dataset (Kaggle or similar)

3.Algorithm Used:
K-Means Clustering Algorithm
It is an unsupervised machine learning algorithm.
It groups data into K clusters based on similarity.
Steps:
Choose number of clusters (K)
Assign data points to nearest cluster center
Update cluster centers
Repeat until convergence

4.Methodology:
Import dataset
Perform data preprocessing
Select features (Income & Spending Score)
Apply K-Means clustering
Visualize clusters using graphs

5. Expected Output:
Customers divided into groups such as:
 High income – High spending
 High income – Low spending
 Low income – High spending
 Low income – Low spending
Graphical visualization of clusters
Insights to help businesses in marketing strategies

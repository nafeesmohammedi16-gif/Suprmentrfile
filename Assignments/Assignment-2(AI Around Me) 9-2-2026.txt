
Assignment Name: AI Around Me
Aim: 
List 10 real-life AI applications you use daily and explain how ML might work behind each.
Objective:
To understand how artificial intelligence is used in daily life by identifying common applications and learning how machine learning works behind them in simple terms.
AI Applications:
1.Google Search:
   Uses machine learning to understand user queries and rank the most relevant web pages based on past data and behavior.
2.YouTube Recommendations:
   ML analyzes watch history, likes, and time spent to suggest videos similar to user interests.
3.Google Maps Navigation:
   Uses ML to predict traffic, suggest fastest routes, and estimate arrival time based on real-time data.
4.Email Spam Filter (Gmail):
   ML models detect patterns in spam emails and automatically filter unwanted messages.
5.Voice Assistants (Google Assistant):
   Uses speech recognition and NLP to understand voice commands and respond intelligently.
6.Face Unlock (Smartphone):
   ML models recognize facial features and compare them with stored data for authentication.
7.Netflix Recommendations:
   ML suggests movies/shows based on viewing history and preferences of similar users.
8.Instagram Feed:
   Uses ML to show posts based on likes, shares, and interaction patterns.
9.Online Shopping (Amazon):
   Recommends products using ML based on search history, purchases, and browsing behavior.
10.Keyboard Autocorrect & Suggestions:
   ML predicts next words and corrects spelling based on typing patterns and language models.


Assignment Name: ML Idea Generator
Aim:
Suggest ML problems in college, healthcare, shopping and describe input → output.
Objective:
To suggest real-world Machine Learning (ML) problems in different domains and clearly define their Input → Output process.

1.College Domain – Student Performance Prediction:
Problem:
Predict whether a student will pass or fail the semester.

Input:
Attendance percentage
Internal marks
Assignment scores
Previous semester CGPA
Study hours per week

Output:
Pass / Fail
OR Predicted Final Grade (A, B, C, etc.)

How ML Helps:
Helps teachers identify weak students early and provide support.

2.Healthcare Domain – Disease Risk Prediction:
Problem:
Predict the risk of diabetes in a patient.

Input:
Age
BMI
Blood sugar level
Blood pressure
Family history
Physical activity level

Output:
High Risk / Medium Risk / Low Risk
OR Probability percentage (e.g., 78% risk)

How ML Helps:
Early detection can prevent serious health complications.

3.Shopping Domain – Product Recommendation System:
Problem:
Recommend products to customers based on their interests.

Input:
Past purchase history
Items viewed
Search history
Cart items
Ratings given

Output:
List of recommended products
“You may also like” suggestions

How ML Helps:
Increases sales and improves customer experience.


Task: Shopping cart:-create a shopping cart using it and remove one item, print total items.

# Create shopping cart
cart = ["Milk", "Bread", "Eggs", "Rice"]

# Add one item
cart.append("Juice")

# Remove one item
cart.remove("Bread")

# Print shopping cart
print("Shopping Cart:", cart)

# Print total items
print("Total Items:", len(cart))

Output:
Shopping Cart: ['Milk', 'Eggs', 'Rice', 'Juice']
Total Items: 4
